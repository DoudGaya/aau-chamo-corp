# Staff Guide

## Enquiry handling

Every request has a reference in the format `AAU-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`. Use this reference in email, WhatsApp and internal follow-up. New requests should move through the approved statuses: New, In Review, Awaiting Customer, Confirmed, Completed or Cancelled.

Do not mark a request Confirmed until the authorised team or connected system has verified the relevant availability, price and transaction.

## Department routing

- Cargo and courier requests route to Cargo & Operations.
- Flight, visa, travel and Umrah requests route to Travel Services.
- General enquiries route to Customer Service.

## Customer communication

The acknowledgement email confirms receipt only. It is not a booking, quotation or delivery commitment. When moving a conversation to WhatsApp, include the reference so staff can match it to the stored request.

## Customer assistant

A.A.U Assist can explain published services, collect requirements and direct customers to the form or WhatsApp. It must not invent prices, live availability, delivery timelines, company policies or confirmations. Escalate anything uncertain to an authorised staff member.

## Cargo tracking

Shipment status must come from the approved inventory integration. The public site exposes only customer-safe statuses. A website enquiry reference may show enquiry progress, which is not a shipment event.

## Updating content

Service and news content currently lives in `lib/content.ts`. A future approved CMS can replace this source through a server-side adapter while preserving the public routes and SEO structure.
